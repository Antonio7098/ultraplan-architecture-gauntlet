### Architecture assessment

**Executive verdict.** The architecture is sound at its core and does not need a second one. Run-control's durable arbitration (fence-verify before mutate; single-row CAS on `current_attempt_id + terminal_outcome IS NULL` with idempotent loser path; reconciliation requiring process-probe evidence keyed on host+boot+PID+birth-token) survived three independent adversarial passes and is the strongest subsystem in the repository. Module-driven composition is correct per doctrine: one shared use-case layer and operation runner feed CLI/TUI/web; web is transport-only under a parser-enforced import test; sprint/study are deep cohesive modules with deliberately different lock/persistence policies; dual JSON+SQLite persistence is coherent per-record (DB-first loads everywhere; JSON mirrors at terminal states). Every confirmed defect lives at a seam where **a policy was copied instead of owned**, and most have already fired: persist-on-outcome re-decided across ~56 sites (two drifted branches found); supervision maintained as two hand-copies with divergent terminal taxonomies; an event-payload vocabulary coordinated by nothing (both most recent commits shipped observability the storage gate deletes); stage vocabularies hand-enumerated ~15 times (two realized misses in opposite directions); and a state-authority move executed hours after the governing workspace forbade it, with no record and no tests.
